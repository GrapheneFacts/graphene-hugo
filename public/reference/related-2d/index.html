Related 2D materials

Evidence: Well-supported that these crystals are studied in the same labs and programmes (Flagship mandate included “related layered materials”: [[timeline/2013]]).

  • hBN — insulating honeycomb; the preferred encapsulant / substrate for clean graphene transport ([[timeline/2010]] Dean).
  • TMDCs (MoS₂, WS₂, …) — many have a band gap; different electronics than graphene.
  • Phosphorene, silicene, MXenes, etc. — separate literatures. Mention when a source actually uses them; do not absorb them into “graphene news.”

Twisted stacks and heterostructures are how these combine ([[timeline/2012]], [[timeline/2018]]).
