Vocabulary

  • ISO/TS 80004-13:2017 — Nanotechnologies — Vocabulary — Part 13: Graphene and related two-dimensional materials. First edition.
  • ISO/TS 80004-13:2024 — Second edition (Sep 2024). Cancels 2017. Adds graphene-related 2D material (GR2M) among other revisions. Joint ISO/IEC circulation in the draft trail.
  • Related core vocab still cited by those parts: ISO/TS 80004-1 (core terms), 80004-2 (nano-objects), 80004-6 (nano-object characterization).

Structural characterization of powders and dispersions

  • ISO/TS 21356-1:2021 (first edition, March 2021). Sequence of methods after isolating flakes: layer number / thickness, lateral size, disorder, layer alignment, specific surface area. Covers graphene, bilayer, nanoplatelets from powder or liquid. Raman, SEM, AFM, TEM, BET in the usual toolkit. CEN adopted as CEN ISO/TS 21356-1:2022.

What a standard is not

Not a bag certificate. Not a health clearance. Not proof a vendor is “ISO graphene certified” because the letters appear on a slide.
