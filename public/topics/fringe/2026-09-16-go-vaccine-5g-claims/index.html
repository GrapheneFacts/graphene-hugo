GO in vaccines / 5G activation claims

Evidence: Fringe

Recycled claims that COVID vaccines contain graphene oxide and/or that GO can be “activated” by 5G. Mid-September 2026 posts repeated 2021-era microscopy and petition-hearing material.

Why Fringe

  • Licensed vaccine ingredient lists (FDA, EMA, MHRA and manufacturers) do not include graphene oxide.
  • GO has been used in lab imaging (e.g. cryo-EM) of spike protein — that is not a formulation ingredient.
  • Viral “self-assembly” clips have been traced by fact-checkers to a pre-COVID high-voltage physics demonstration.
