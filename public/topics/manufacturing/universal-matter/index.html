What it is

Universal Matter Inc., formed Jul 2019. Process: Rice / Tour Flash Joule Heating to turbostratic graphene from carbon streams (petcoke, ag waste, recycled plastic/rubber in their wording). Genable dispersions for coatings / asphalt / concrete in later copy.

Sites they name

  • Burlington, ON demonstration plant: 1320 Heine Court, Burlington, Ontario L7L 6L9 on a 2024 financing note. 2023 acquisition release: 18,000 sq ft building; nameplate ~1 t/day if fully utilized — company plan, Q3 2023 target in that note.
  • Houston: 900 South Loop West (suite numbers wander in directories).
  • UK: Redcar / Teesside via the AGM purchase — Applied Graphene Materials UK + US ops, US$1.3 million, completion 28 Mar 2023. AGM plc later renamed Universal Matter GBR in trade copy.

Tour flash graphene ≠ a 2004 flake foundry. AGM Genable book sits under this name now.
