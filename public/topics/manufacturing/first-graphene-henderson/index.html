First Graphene — Henderson plant

Evidence: Documented plant location and product name in ASX copy. Capacity “up to 100 t/year” is company-stated.

Ticker: ASX FGR. Parent list: [[topics/manufacturing/directory]]

Site

  • Primary mill: Henderson, near Perth, Western Australia.
  • UK: First Graphene (UK) Ltd / Manchester R&D and marketing, not the tonne line.

Product

PureGRAPH nanoplatelets as powder, aqueous dispersion, custom dispersion, masterbatch.

Company-stated scale

Up to 100 tonnes per year at Henderson (2026 company/Proactive summary of the presentation pack).

Downstream that is not the mill

  • Pacific Urethanes PO for mining PU (logged on this site).
  • Breedon graphene-enhanced cement trials; FP McCann roof-tile runs using that cement (ASX, Dec 2025–Aug 2026). Those tonnes are cement, not extra graphene capacity.

Regulatory lines they publish

REACH (EU/UK) and Australian industrial-chemicals registration claimed; US EPA process described as underway in 2026 investor copy.

What this is not

Not a 4,000 t-class powder plant. Not a graphite mine.
