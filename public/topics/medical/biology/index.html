Theories in circulation

  • “Pencil lead, therefore biology is done.” Ignores GO vs monolayer vs powder. [[reference/misconceptions]]
  • “One IC50 in a dish predicts a workplace or a vaccine.” Wrong evidence class. [[topics/fringe]]
  • Oxidative-stress / sharp-edge cutting as the mechanism. Real papers exist; they do not collapse to one slogan.
  • Bioaccumulation as a cover-up story. Documented on [[logs/conspiracies]], not here as fact.

Projections (forecast language)

Reviews keep projecting biodegradable graphene, DNA–graphene scaffolds, and “green” GO from biomass. Treat as slides until there is an NCT number or a regulator text. Market-size “graphene healthcare” figures stay [[logs/unsourced]] unless the house and the definition are quoted.
