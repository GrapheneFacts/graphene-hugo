Goals people write down

  • A cortical or peripheral electrode that records or stimulates without a stainless-steel penalty in flexibility or site area.
  • A scaffold that is conductive enough for cells and resorbable enough for a surgeon.
  • A wearable (tattoo, textile, microneedle) that lasts a shift, not a press cycle.
  • Closed-loop interfaces. Still a review-article sentence in 2025–26.

Theories in circulation

  • “Graphene replaces silicon in diagnostics this decade.” Same foundry problem as the electronics pitch. [[logs/theories]]
  • A patterning paper “will treat” a named disease this cycle. Motivation language. [[logs/unsourced]]
  • Long-term implant / IV GO is solved. 2025–26 reviews still list cytotoxicity, dose, metals, and fate as open.

Projections (forecast language)

Patient-specific printed grafts, smart microneedles, injectable graphene, AI-designed scaffolds — review-article futures. They sit next to the NCT table, not in place of it.
