  • China plant-capacity headlines become common; treat tonnes as company-stated unless a regulator text agrees. [[reference/standards]]

This decade is when “graphene market will be $X billion by 20YY” slides multiply. Those forecasts are [[logs/unsourced]] unless we quote the exact house and definition.

2020–2025 — shakeout language

  • Some AIM names shrink, rename, or sell assets. AGM later leaves the “standalone graphene AIM” set in industry talk (confirm any sale year on a dedicated page before stating it as fact).
  • Haydale US ceramics subsidiary Chapter 11 in early 2025 appears in 2026 secondary roundups (Preliminary until the filing is attached).
  • Product-line resets: heating panels, coatings, cement additives, oil sorbents — graphene as an ingredient, not a CMOS replacement. [[reference/applications]]
