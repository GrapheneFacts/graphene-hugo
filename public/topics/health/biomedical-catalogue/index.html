A 2026 review of GO nanomaterials stated there were still no Phase I/II trials of GO as a therapeutic (drug / imaging agent / scaffold product) in the sense of a graphene-oxide medicine. FDA/EMA review graphene case-by-case under general nanomaterial rules, not a special “graphene pathway.”

Research successes (not the same as products)

These are real papers or methods. They are successes of measurement or fabrication.

  • Conductive scaffolds and oriented GO/PCL fibres: rat sciatic-gap models and in-vitro axon length reports (e.g. 2024 IJN oriented GO/PCL). Animal. Preliminary translation.
  • GO as a high-area carrier for small molecules in cells and rodents (dexamethasone, anti-PD tool compounds, photothermal ideas). Delivery papers, not approved formulations. 2025 Pharmaceuticals review maps this pile and names toxicity / metabolism as the brake.
  • Graphene / GO electrodes and GFET sensors: high surface area, flexibility. 2024 Biosensors and Bioelectronics neuro review is typical: many disease names in the abstract, few cleared instruments.
  • Holey graphene as a molecular stencil on gold (Weiss / UCLA 2015): patterning chemistry toward sensors, not a neural implant. [[posts/holey-graphene-molecular-stencil]]
  • Antibacterial / antiviral in-vitro GO literature: large, condition-dependent, easy to overclaim.

Failures, stalls, and honest brakes

Claim that circulated What the record supports
Graphene will be in licensed human vaccines as an ingredient Ingredient lists do not include GO. Fringe. [[logs/conspiracies]]
GO adjuvant papers in animals prove a human shot recipe Different evidence class
Replace silicon diagnostic chips this decade Same band-gap / foundry problem as electronics. [[logs/theories]]
A patterning or coating paper “will treat” a named disease this cycle Motivation language. [[logs/unsourced]]
One toxicity paper ⇒ covert deployment Non sequitur. [[reference/safety]]
Long-term implant / IV GO is “solved” Reviews in 2025–26 still list cytotoxicity, dose, metals, and fate as open

Rodent high-dose GO can inflame lung and accumulate; that is in the nanosafety pile. It does not license either slogan (“safe” / “weapon”).

Projections (labeled as projections)

Review articles project: injectable / biodegradable graphene, AI-designed DNA–graphene scaffolds, patient-specific printed grafts, smart microneedles, closed-loop neural interfaces.

Treat those sentences as forecast language. They belong next to the trial table, not in place of it. Market-size slides for “graphene healthcare” are [[logs/unsourced]] unless the house and the definition of “graphene product” are quoted.

How this folder will grow

New row needs: form (GO / rGO / CVD / composite), species or NCT number, endpoint, and a primary. Niche is in. “Cures X” without a denominator is out.
