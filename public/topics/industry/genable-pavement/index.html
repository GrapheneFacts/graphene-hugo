Genable Pavement = UM graphene additive in asphalt (ex-AGM UK / Redcar).

Named lays (council 3 Mar 2025 + later APSE slide):

  • Flatts Lane Country Park entrance (Redcar & Cleveland) — first UK public road in that press; Tarmac Coxhoe plant; ~150–200 t asphalt depending on the writeup
  • Pennyman Walk, Marske-by-the-Sea — ~180 t
  • US/Canada car parks and NCAT test-track language on UM product page; Minnesota Route 12 “3-mile section” in the APSE slide

Company product page: 3.0 wt% of asphalt cement, dry add, “two PG bumps / 12 °C” vs SBS talk; NCAT acceptance of a section (May 2024 Phase 1; pave fall 2024; monitor into 2026). Those metrics stay company/slide until the NCAT table is attached.
