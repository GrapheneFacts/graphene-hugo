Parent: [[reference/applications]] · [[topics/manufacturing/gmg]]

What it is

THERMAL-XR / THERMAL-XR ENHANCE: water-based acrylic with GMG graphene, sold as a coat for aluminium and copper air-cooled heat exchangers (RTPF and microchannel in product copy). Job they state: rebuild heat transfer on corroded coils, protect new ones, cut compressor work.

What they published

  • 9 Jul 2026 GMG release: >30,000 h ASTM B117-19 salt spray, no observed corrosion, external US lab (Industrial Inspection & Analysis in later product copy). Company comparison to 1,000–2,000 h class coatings is theirs.
  • Product site: heat-transfer work with University of Queensland; energy-savings tests at an ISO-accredited lab — attach those PDFs before treating % cuts as Well-supported.
  • Graphene-Info (radar): June 2026 order talk ~A$400k and a blending plant — Preliminary until the GMG release PDF is on the raise/order tape.

A coil coat is not a graphene battery and not a 4,000 t mill.
