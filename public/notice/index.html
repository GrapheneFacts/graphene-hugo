What this site is

A public, labeled archive of graphene-family materials: forms, history, papers, plants, money notices, occupational and biomedical records, and (separately) circulating rumors. Short notes may still appear on @GrapheneFacts. The durable copy is here.

What this site is not

Not medical advice. Not investment advice. Not legal advice. Not a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, inhale, implant, or ingest anything. Not a newsletter, handbook shop, or competitor to trade portals.

How confidence is marked

Folder is for browsing. The label on a claim is for confidence.

Label Means
Well-supported Peer-reviewed paper, regulator text, or confirmed order
Preliminary Preprint, company lab, pilot, or company-stated tonnes
Unverified / Unsourced No solid primary
Fringe Plot-style narrative, documented only

Unsourced is not automatically Fringe. Detail: [[guides/evidence-labels]]. Trade portals are radar, not the shelf: [[guides/sources]]. Figures: [[topics/finance/how-we-log-figures]].
