2011–2012 — Post-Nobel acceleration

Evidence: Well-supported at program level

CVD on copper plus transfer methods become the default film story. Flexible electronics, RF transistors, and photonics demos multiply. EU policy talk hardens toward a Flagship ([[timeline/2013]]).

The era’s idea — graphene replaces silicon — collides with band gap, contact resistance, and uniformity. File that ideal on [[logs/theories]]. Do not treat a 2011 keynote as a 2026 product.
