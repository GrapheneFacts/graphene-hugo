Twist / proximity

  • Zhou et al., Nature 613, 268 (2023). Bernal bilayer graphene + WSe₂: SC at zero field, Tc up ~10× vs the fragile ~30 mK window without WSe₂ in that comparison; density window ~8× wider. Only when the field pushes holes toward WSe₂ (Ising SOC story).
  • Su, Kuiri, et al., Nat. Mater. 22, 1332 (2023). Twisted double bilayer + WSe₂ superconducts in small pockets at 1.24° and 1.37°. TDBG without the dichalcogenide had been the missing cousin.
