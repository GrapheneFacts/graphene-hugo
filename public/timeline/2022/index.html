Twist

  • Park / Cao / Jarillo-Herrero line, Nat. Mater. 21, 884 (2022) and the MIT “family” note: alternating-twist quadrilayer (and the five-layer cousin) host correlated and superconducting pockets near new magic angles (~1.68° class for ATQG). Family, not a SKU.
  • Kim et al., Nature 606, 494 (2022). STM/STS on magic-angle trilayer: reconstruction toward mirror-symmetric stacking; coherence peaks; doping window that looks like a gap-to-nodal crossover in that paper’s language.
