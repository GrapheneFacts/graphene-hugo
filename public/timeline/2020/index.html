Twist

  • Stepanov, Das, Lu, et al., Nat. Phys. 16, 926 (2020). Superconductivity in magic-angle bilayer can sit away from the correlated insulators; SC survives larger angle detuning. Competing-phases reading, not “doped Mott like cuprates only.”
  • Cao / Jarillo-Herrero 2020 Nature pair (same week MIT notes): mapping local twist disorder, and a four-layer magic-angle stack more sensitive to E and B than the bilayer. Still a fridge device.

Twisted trilayer Pauli-limit story is 2021. Do not collapse the years.
