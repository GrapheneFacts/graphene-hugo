Science

  • Thomas et al., ACS Nano 9, 10909 (2015) — holey graphene as a molecular stencil on gold. [[posts/holey-graphene-molecular-stencil]]
  • Kim, Yu, Kim, et al., Nat. Nanotechnol. 10, 676 (2015). Electrically biased suspended graphene emits bright visible light. Hot electrons ~2,800 K localized because the substrate heat sink is gone; ~1,000-fold thermal-radiation efficiency vs on-SiO₂ mid-IR emitters in that comparison. CVD arrays shown. A lab emitter, not a display SKU.
