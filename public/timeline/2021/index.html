Twist

Cao, Park, Watanabe, Taniguchi, Jarillo-Herrero, Nature 595, 526 (2021). Magic-angle twisted trilayer graphene: superconductivity in in-plane fields >10 T, about 2–3× the conventional Pauli limit in that report; re-entrant SC at high field over a narrower density/displacement window. Not predicted from strong spin–orbit. Still three sheets in a fridge. arXiv 2103.12083 (22 Mar); journal 21 Jul.
