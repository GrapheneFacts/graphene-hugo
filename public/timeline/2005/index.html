GrapheneFacts 2005

2005

Evidence: Well-supported for the core experiments. Interpretations (“relativistic electrons in a pencil mark”) are pedagogy, not extra data.

Documented science

  • Novoselov, Geim, Morozov, Jiang, Katsnelson, Grigorieva, Dubonos, Firsov, Nature 438, 197 (2005) — two-dimensional gas of massless Dirac fermions in graphene.
  • Zhang, Tan, Stormer, Kim, Nature 438, 201 (2005) — experimental observation of the quantum Hall effect and Berry’s phase in graphene.

Together with [[timeline/2004]], these papers are why Wallace’s 1947 bands ([[timeline/1947]]) stop being a graphite approximation and become a measured monolayer spectrum.

Claims in the air

Popular write-ups already say graphene will transform electronics. The measurements are magnetotransport on small cleaved flakes. No foundry process exists.

Open

How to grow or print large monolayers without destroying mobility. That problem dominates the next four years.
