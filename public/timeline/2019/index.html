Twist follow-on

  • Yoo, Engelke, et al., Nat. Mater. 18, 448 (2019). Atomic reconstruction in twisted bilayer graphene; crossover near θc ≈ 1° from incommensurate moiré to commensurate domains with soliton walls.
  • Chen, Sharpe, Gallagher, et al., Nature 572, 215 (2019). ABC trilayer graphene / hBN moiré: Mott-like states at 1/4 and 1/2 filling below ~20 K; signatures of tunable superconductivity under a displacement field. Not magic-angle bilayer; a different stack.
  • Mid-2019 STM / spectroscopy cluster on magic-angle bilayer (Kerelsky, Xie, Jiang in Nature; Choi preprint) — maps the flat-band correlations the 2018 transport papers announced.

Still two sheets (or three) in a fridge. Not a tote.

Industry texture

Reviews: graphene works as additive or electrode film, not as silicon. Powder tox literature accumulates; that is not a conspiracy. Later NIOSH field work sits on that stack.

Unchanged bottlenecks: dispersion, grade, 1 Ah cell → pack.
