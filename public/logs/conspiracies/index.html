Conspiracy and fringe-narrative log

Not a truth list. Documenting a start date is not endorsing the claim.

Two different problems live on this tab:

  1. Fringe — a conspiracy-style narrative (plot, hidden ingredient, activation, cover-up).
  2. Unsourced — a factual-sounding claim with no solid primary source. It may be ordinary hype, a rumor, or a press sentence that cannot be opened. It is not automatically a conspiracy.

Theories and companies stay elsewhere: [[logs/theories]] · [[logs/companies]].
