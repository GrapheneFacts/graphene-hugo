What “Unsourced” means here

The [[guides/evidence-labels|Unverified]] label on a news item: assertion without a solid primary source.

This page is the standing list of those sentences — vendor talk, forecast talk, medical talk, anonymous screenshots — so they have a home that is not “secretly true” and not “automatically a conspiracy.”

Unsourced Fringe
Missing source Story about hidden actors, secret ingredients, remote activation, cover-up
May become Preliminary if a paper or filing appears Stays Fringe until the narrative changes, even if one footnote is real
Often ordinary hype Often recycled as a package

Archive folder for file drops: topics/unverified/ (public repo).

How a row leaves this page

  • Primary paper, filing, or regulator text appears → retag Preliminary or Well-supported and file under the right topic.
  • The same sentence is absorbed into a cover-up story → add a Fringe row on [[logs/conspiracies]]; keep this Unsourced row if the source is still missing.
  • Shown to be a recurrence of an old sourced paper → [[recurrence/log]].

Niche unsourced claims are welcome. Silence is not a source.
