Scientific & Technical Developments

1. Graphene stencil for placing molecules on gold

Summary: UCLA (Paul Weiss et al.) used a graphene sheet with tiny holes as a mask on gold so molecules attach only where intended. Published in ACS Nano.
Sources: UCLA Health release
Evidence: Well-supported

2. Water dissociation at graphene vacancies (computational)

Summary: Machine-learned potentials show solvation changes how water breaks at a graphene single vacancy. arXiv 2609.11602.
Sources: arXiv:2609.11602
Evidence: Preliminary (preprint, simulation)

3. Graphene fibres via viscous flow of graphene oxide

Summary: Nature Materials news & views on polymer-like viscoelasticity of GO in glycerol enabling ultrahigh draw ratios in wet spinning.
Sources: Nature Materials
Evidence: Well-supported

4. Graphene zone-plate optical PUF

Summary: Graphene diffraction zone plates + RGB light challenges + a transformer model for hardware authentication. Opto-Electronic Advances.
Evidence: Well-supported (published optics paper; press is promotional)

5. Graphene oxide dew-point / icing sensor (patent)

Summary: Stony Brook / SUNY patented a GO-film method for dew point; aviation icing interest.
Evidence: Preliminary (patent / tech-transfer)

6. Few-layer graphene via cascaded centrifugation

Summary: Scientific Reports: LPE + stepped centrifuge speeds; ~5–12 layers.
Sources: Scientific Reports
Evidence: Well-supported

7. Graphene-enhanced carbon-fibre UD tape (lab scale)

Summary: CompSTLar / Aimplas: continuous UD tapes with graphene/CNT-doped LMPAEK matrix.
Evidence: Preliminary

Industry & Commercial News

8. GMG G®CELLS: 489 cycles at 10C with no measured fade

Summary: 1 Ah cells charged and discharged in ~6 minutes; no measured capacity loss after 489 cycles (as of 4 Sep). LTO under the same profile fell to ~86% by 64 cycles. Energy density ~39 Wh/kg at this rate (company says unoptimized).
Sources: GMG announcement
Evidence: Preliminary (third-party lab cycling on small cells)

Regulatory / Policy

15. NIOSH workplace graphene exposure measurements

Summary: 44 workers at 11 US sites. Against a conservative band of 10 µg/m³, many samples exceeded it when dry powder was handled. Prefer masterbatch/liquid. 10 µg/m³ is a band, not a formal OSHA PEL.
Evidence: Well-supported occupational hygiene data
